## Store listing in English (en-US)

**Product name:** PdfPaper

**Short description:**

> A complete, subscription-free PDF editor: OCR to make scans searchable, text editing that matches the
> document's font, images, page management, compression and true redaction. Runs entirely on your PC.

**Description:**

> PdfPaper is a free, open-source PDF editor for Windows, built for people who handle official
> documents, scans and paperwork every day.
>
> MAKE SCANS SEARCHABLE
> Built-in OCR (Italian, English and more) turns scanned PDFs into documents you can search, select and
> copy from, while keeping their original look.
>
> EDIT TEXT SEAMLESSLY
> Add or correct text: PdfPaper suggests the same font, size and color as the document. In digital PDFs
> it reads the original font; in scans it estimates it from the image.
>
> TRUE REDACTION
> Black out names, addresses and personal data: the content under the area is actually removed from the
> file, not just covered. Choose the background and label colors.
>
> PAGES, IMAGES AND COMPRESSION
> Insert blank pages, pages from other PDFs or images as new pages; delete, move and rotate pages by 90°
> or 180° in one click. Add signatures, stamps and logos. Shrink files for e-mail.
>
> PRIVACY
> No account and no internet connection needed: your documents never leave your computer.
>
> PdfPaper is free software under the GNU AGPL v3 license. Source code is available on GitHub.

**Product features:**

1. Built-in OCR to make scanned PDFs searchable
2. Add and edit text with the document's own font, size and color
3. True redaction: content under the area is removed from the file
4. Custom colors for redaction background and label
5. Insert images, signatures and stamps
6. Insert, delete, move and rotate pages (90° and 180°)
7. PDF compression with four levels
8. Text selection with copy, cut and paste (images too)
9. Works offline with no account: documents stay on your PC
10. Free and open source
